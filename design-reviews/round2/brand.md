# Brand and visual identity review: Mizan (v2)

## Score: 68/100 (round 1: 61)
It is now a credible, restrained institutional identity: lapis ink, Plex Serif headings, the Kufi mark at hero scale and a cleaner 3D valley. But it has traded "AI warm-paper template" for "tasteful private-bank template", and the 3D is still a set of props rather than a crafted cut-paper world.

## What works
- **Lapis is now an owned colour, and the system is tighter.** `--lapis:#1E3A5F`, brass kept to the seal and active marker, statuses kept separate (`global.css:1-12`). Buttons, segmented controls, the seal, `§` numerals and the Arabic ayah all share one ink (`desktop/00-y0.png`, `07-y5670.png`, `09-y7290.png`). The page reads as one hand.
- **The Arabic now leads the brand.** The ميزان Kufi mark sits at hero scale with the gloss "Mizan, the balance" (`desktop/00-y0.png`). The ayah moved out of the dark footer onto paper, set in lapis Naskh with ornamented verse markers and a serif translation (`desktop/09-y7290.png`). This is the most distinctive and respectful moment on the site.
- **The headings have a voice.** IBM Plex Serif 500 for H1, H2, record names and big dates (`global.css:37-39`) adds a documentary, scholarly tone that Plex Sans lacked. "1 Jan 2028" in serif on the sunset panel (`desktop/05-y4050.png`) feels like a register entry.
- **The 3D art direction is clearly better.** The cartoon clouds and palms are gone. The gates are a single stone tone instead of ochre and black. Cut-paper lattice screens with khatam (8-point star) holes now line the road (`desktop/03-y2430.png`, left), which is exactly the right vocabulary. The night state with lit arched windows is still strong (`desktop/05-y4050.png`).
- **The static fallback now has an identity.** The arch-framed star lattice behind the scale (`desktop-no3d/00-y0.png`, `mobile/00-y0.png`) is a real emblem, not a placeholder icon. The seal comes in two drawings: a small one without ring text (`desktop/02-y1620.png` footer) and a full ring for the record sheet (`desktop/modal.png`).

## Problems

- **High: the identity is still mostly a set of safe defaults.** Cream paper, navy/lapis, Plex Serif with Plex Sans, 3px corners, hairline rules and soft-shadow cards. That is the standard "trustworthy fintech/legal" kit. Remove the Kufi mark and ayah, and `desktop-no3d/02-y1620.png` or `07-y5670.png` could belong to a wealth manager or a compliance SaaS. One superfamily is tidy but cautious. Nothing in the layout system (grid, rules, numbering, margins) is specific to a register or a scholarly document; the system lives only in the motifs.
- **High: the 3D still reads as assembled props.** Minarets with balconies, crenellated walls and a domed building still form the skyline (`desktop/04-y3240.png`, `05-y4050.png`). The tone is better than v1 but still "generic Islamic town". The arches, hourglass and scale are flat Lambert or glossy Standard materials on boxy geometry. The paper-cut idea shows only in the lattice screens; the rest has no cut edges, layering or paper thickness. The hourglass glass is a grey smear in day light (`desktop/04-y3240.png`), and at night it becomes a dark blob filling a third of the frame (`desktop/05-y4050.png`).
- **High: the hero's 3D frame is still unresolved.** The scale is cropped by the right edge of the viewport, with the right pan cut off (`desktop/00-y0.png`). A grey ghost arch still bleeds through the paper fade at about x=720-830. The caption "The scale is level only while an approval holds today." sits in a floating white box at the bottom, unanchored to anything. The static version of the same hero (`desktop-no3d/00-y0.png`) is now more composed than the 3D one, which should not happen.
- **Med: transitions between the 3D and document sections look broken.** In `desktop/02-y1620.png` the register ends and a blurred road plane with smeared shadow bars rises behind §2. In `desktop/06-y4860.png` the night scene fades into paper as a grey haze band behind the bottom of the §3 panel. Both read as rendering artefacts rather than deliberate edges. A hard cut-paper edge (a deckled or arched mask) would fix both.
- **Med: the pinned panels have dead space and collisions.** The §2 panel keeps about 200px of blank sheet under step 5 before §3 slides in (`desktop/04-y3240.png`). On the timeline, "Ends 12 Oct 2027" and "Feb 2028" crowd each other at different baselines (`desktop/04-y3240.png`, `05-y4050.png`). These are small, but they are polish failures in the brand's signature interaction.
- **Med: mobile lost the 3D entirely and gained some rough edges.** Under 760px the page is always static (`App.tsx:29`), so phones never see the sunset-to-night device, the best brand idea. That is defensible, but then the static `SunArc` has to carry the idea, and it is a thin dashed arc with a dot (`mobile/06-y4558.png`). The type filter wraps with "Yield Vault" alone on a second row inside broken borders (`mobile/02-y1519.png`). The section nav strip is clipped mid-word ("Who d") with no fade cue (`mobile/00-y0.png`).
- **Low: the wordmark is still doubled.** The nav shows the star-scale mark plus "Mizan" in serif, and the hero shows ميزان plus a sentence. The nav no longer includes the Arabic, so the lockup is cleaner, but the Latin "Mizan" in Plex Serif 600 is generic next to the Kufi. A drawn Latin wordmark, or using only the Kufi word in the nav, would be stronger.
- **Low: the dark code block is still a generic code-editor island** (`desktop/07-y5670.png`). It is now the darkest element on the page (the footer is shorter), and it ignores the lapis system: it is ink-black, not lapis-black.
- **Low: the strikethrough "Approved" in red for Withdrawn** (`desktop/02-y1620.png`) is the only place a struck word appears. It is visually loud next to the quiet "not current" annotations. It is effective, but it breaks the register's calm typographic register.

## Top 5 recommendations (ranked by score impact)

1. **Commit the 3D fully to cut paper.** Replace the minarets, crenellations and box buildings with 3-5 layered paper planes per depth band: arches, lattice screens and a mashrabiya-like skyline silhouette, each an extruded `Shape` 0.1-0.2 units thick with a lighter bevel edge and a soft offset shadow on the plane behind. Make the scale and hourglass matte paper/brass cutouts in the same language. Reuse `latticeShape`, which already proves the approach works (`world.ts:243-249`).
2. **Fix the hero composition.** Pull the camera back or move the scale left so both pans sit inside the frame. Replace the gradient fade with a hard arched or deckled paper mask that echoes the static emblem's arch, so text sits on solid paper and the 3D sits in a window. Put the caption on a fixed baseline under the window, not in a floating box.
3. **Build a layout signature, not just motifs.** Give the document sections a register grid: a narrow left margin column carrying `§` numbers, record IDs and dates in tabular figures, hairline column rules, and a consistent marginalia position for notes. Run the same grid through the register table, §4 and §5, so the page looks like one bound document. This is the move that would make it unmistakable without any illustration.
4. **Give mobile its own version of the signature moment.** Draw a richer static sunset: the emblem arch whose sky fills from dawn to night as the date slider moves, with windows lighting up at expiry. Fix the filter wrap (use a horizontal-scroll segmented control or a select on narrow screens) and add a fade or overflow cue to the section strip.
5. **Make the hard edges between scenes deliberate, and tune the material.** Use a cut-paper edge where the 3D starts and stops (`desktop/02-y1620.png`, `06-y4860.png`). Give the hourglass glass a paper-tinted fresnel or drop the glass for a cut silhouette. Set the code block to lapis-ink (`#14243A`-ish) so it joins the palette.

## What would get this to 85+
The palette, type and Arabic now belong together, so the remaining gap is craft and specificity. The 3D needs to become a true layered cut-paper world built from the lattice and arch language it has already started, with a composed hero frame and deliberate edges. The document sections need a layout signature (a register grid with marginalia and tabular `§` and record columns) so the identity holds even without illustration. Then carry the sunset idea to mobile in a drawn form; with that, Mizan would look like a designed institution rather than a careful template.
