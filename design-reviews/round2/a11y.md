# Mizan: mobile, accessibility and performance review (round 2)

**Score: 79/100** (round 1: 73). This is now strong, well-built accessible work. Every High and most Medium findings from round 1 are fixed, and the measurements confirm it. What still holds it back: phones get a long page under a tall fixed header, the pinned §2 panel still clips on common short laptop screens, and the record dialog is long and has a few rough edges.

## What works

- **The hero contrast failure is gone.** I hid the text and sampled the pixels behind it again. `.hero .lede` now has a minimum of 6.92:1 at 1440×900 and 8.26:1 at 1280 and 1024, with 0% of pixels below 4.5:1 at every width (round 1: minimum 2.06, 8% below). The gradient is now solid paper to 50% and the column is capped at `calc(50vw - 64px)` (`global.css`, `.hero-grid`). The hero links measure at least 9.22:1. The 3D caption has its own 0.92-alpha backing.
- **Phones get the same content as desktop, and no three.js.** `useMedia('(max-width: 760px), (max-height: 520px)')` (`App.tsx`) sends phones and landscape phones to the still page. The ledes, the roles list and the date slider are all back. On mobile, the network log shows only `index.js` (88 KB gzip) and CSS: the 128 KB three.js chunk is never requested. The landscape clipping from round 1 (844×390) is gone, because that size is now a normal-flow document.
- **Control borders and announcements are fixed.** `--rule-ctl #8A8170` measures 3.63:1 on sheet, 3.85:1 on white and 3.30:1 on paper, and it is used on the search box, `select`, `.seg`, `.steps`, `.jumps` and the year bar. "Showing N of M records" is in `role="status"` (`Register.tsx`). The hero uses a short sr-only `role=status` sentence instead of a card-wide live region.
- **Dialog and keyboard.** The background `.page` gets `inert` while a record is open (verified `inert=true`). The dialog title is a real `h2` that receives focus, with `h3` sections below it. Tab is trapped, and Esc closes the dialog, clears the hash and returns focus to "Open record MZN-001". The Close button sits in a sticky bar. Records have their own URL (`#record/MZN-004`), and Back closes them. The focus ring is now lapis: 9.85:1 on paper, and sheet on the footer at 15.5:1. Tabbing through 40 stops at 320×640 never put focus under the nav or off-screen.
- **Navigation and semantics.** Below 1080px the section links stay visible as a scrolling second row. There is a footer nav, and "Demo" is shown at every width. In 3D mode all five steps stay in the DOM (`.step-stack`), so screen readers get every step. Code samples are `role=region` with a label. axe-core (WCAG 2.2 AA plus best practice) found 0 violations on the page with the legend open and an empty filter result, and with the dialog open at 390 and 1440.
- **Resilience and cost.** `webglcontextlost` now falls back to the still page. The WebGL test context is released straight away. The grain layer no longer uses a blend mode. CLS is 0.003 on mobile and 0.005 on desktop.

## Top 5 recommendations (ranked by score impact)

1. **Cut the phone page to about 9 screens.** On ≤640px: put §5's matrix, fields and verify steps behind `<details>` (keep the Solidity sample and one sentence). Collapse the "Characterisation / Covered / Screens / Provenance" blocks in the dialog into `<details>` sections. Merge "Next steps" into the footer. Show §4's seats as a compact list. Target under 8,000px at 390.
2. **Measure the header instead of hard-coding it, and shrink it on phones.** Set `--nav-h` from a `ResizeObserver` on `.nav`, so scroll padding and sticky offsets always match. On ≤420px, drop the header button to an icon-plus-label or move it into the link row, keeping the header at about 56px plus a 40px link row. In landscape (`max-height:520px`), hide the link row or make the header `position:static`.
3. **Fix the pinned panel on short screens.** Either raise the still-page cutoff to `(max-height: 700px)`, or keep `.sticky` and add `max-height: calc(100svh - var(--nav-h) - 32px); overflow:auto`, or size `.step-stack` to the current step instead of the tallest. Re-test at 800×560, 1024×600 and 1280×600.
4. **Polish the dialog.** Start the focus cycle at Close (or put the title before the bar in the DOM), and treat index -1 as "before first" so Shift+Tab goes to Close. Make the rule buttons a full-width 1-column or 3-equal-column grid. Replace the dead PDF link with plain text: "Report: MZN-001.pdf (not published in the demo)".
5. **Prerender the HTML and slim the fonts.** Prerender the HTML at build time (for example `vite-plugin-prerender` or a small `renderToString` step) so the hero paints before React loads. Self-host and preload the Plex Sans 400 and Serif 500 woff2 files with `size-adjust` fallbacks to remove the swap repaint. That should bring mobile LCP under 2.5s. Also give the brass nav underline a 3:1 colour (for example `--lapis`), and add a right-edge fade to the mobile link row.

## What would get this to 85+

Accessibility is close to AA-clean. What's left is making the phone experience fit: a page of 9 screens or fewer at 390px under a header no taller than about 100px whose height feeds the scroll offsets, and a dialog that opens on a summary with the detail collapsed. Fix the pinned §2 panel at 560–700px heights and the dialog's Tab order, and ship prerendered HTML, and this would be an excellent, minor-issues-only result.
