# Brand and visual identity review: Mizan (v1)

## Score: 61/100
A disciplined, coherent document system with one strong idea (expiry as sunset into night), but it looks like the default "warm parchment + ink + brass + Plex" palette, and the 3D valley uses postcard Orientalism instead of an identity of its own.

## What works
- **Status colour discipline.** Green, amber, red, blue and grey appear only on review statuses (`global.css:2-4`, `.t-ok`…`.t-mute`). The register (`desktop/06-y4860.png`) and rule matrix (`desktop/09-y7290.png`) are calm and quick to scan. Most crypto sites fail at this; here it reads as a system, not decoration.
- **The sunset-to-night metaphor is the best brand idea on the page.** In `desktop/04-y3240.png` → `05-y4050.png` the valley moves from day to dusk to night with lit windows as Active turns to Expired. It is a real narrative device tied to the product's core concept (a conclusion that stays while its validity ends). The static `SunArc` (`desktop-no3d/02-y1620.png`) carries the same idea.
- **The notarial-register restraint mostly holds.** Hairline rules, `§` section numerals in brass ink, no mono eyebrows, no gradient candy buttons, one grain layer. The modal (`desktop/modal.png`) reads like a record sheet, and the rotated, multiplied ink seal is a nice touch.
- **The Arabic is handled with care.** Reem Kufi is subset to the single wordmark ميزان (`index.html:13`). The footer ayah (Al-Rahman 55:7-9) is set in Naskh with a sober citation and a gloss of the name (`desktop/10-y8100.png`). This is the most dignified moment on the site.
- **The 8-point star (khatam) motif runs through the system:** mark, favicon, seal, gate step badges, bullets in "Independence", and night stars in the SunArc. It gives the identity one repeatable unit.

## Problems

- **High: The palette and type are the stock "AI premium" combination.** Parchment `#F3EBDD`, near-black navy ink, brass `#B8893F`, IBM Plex Sans in 600 for headlines, and a sheet card with a soft long shadow (`global.css:7-16`). Remove the 3D and the logo, and `desktop-no3d/00-y0.png` could be any fintech or legal-tech template. The headline is plain Plex Sans Semibold with tight tracking, so it has no voice. Nothing in the type says "register", "ledger" or "Islamic scholarship". The only distinctive type (Reem Kufi) is 22px in the nav.
- **High: The 3D art direction falls into Orientalist postcard clichés.** Minarets, onion domes, palm trees, ogee arches, a sand road and stacked semicircle "cartoon" clouds (`desktop/00-y0.png`, `03-y2430.png`, `05-y4050.png`). Onion domes are Mughal or Russian, not the Arabian/Andalusian vocabulary the arches suggest. For a serious Sharia governance product aimed at scholars, "desert bazaar town" is the wrong register. It reads as theme-park Arabia, not institutional trust. The scholarly, archival, geometric tradition (girih, muqarnas, manuscript rulings, waqf deeds) is where the brand should live.
- **High: Hero composition is muddy.** The left-to-right paper wash (`global.css:108`, `.mode-3d .hero::before`) leaves a ghosted arch and wall at about 40% opacity directly behind the lede and record card (`desktop/00-y0.png`). The scale sits on a brown plinth against a grey-brown wall with similar values, so the hero object does not separate. "Evidence" and "Policy" are flat white rectangle sprites with system-looking labels that read like debug overlays, not crafted labels. The first frame is the brand's handshake, and it looks unresolved.
- **Med: The gates' colours fight the palette.** The first arch is a flat mustard/ochre (`desktop/01-y810.png`, `02-y1620.png`) that is neither the brass accent nor the paper tones, followed by pure black arches. The material break (flat ochre slab against glossy gold scale and hourglass, `world.ts:307`, `:394`) makes the scene feel assembled from parts rather than art-directed. The "paper-cut" promise only half lands: walls are Lambert boxes with a paper texture, not layered cut paper with visible edges and offset shadows.
- **Med: Large dead planes in the 3D frames.** In `desktop/01-y810.png` and `02-y1620.png`, a huge dark wall slab fills the left third behind the §1 panel, and the lower 40% is flat road. The camera stations frame the gate well but leave the rest unconsidered. On mobile (`mobile/02-y1519.png`) the black arches form a heavy mass with no detail.
- **Med: The seal is illegible at most sizes.** The text-on-path is 7.4 units in a 120 viewBox (`Seal.tsx:15`). At the 56px register-footer size (`desktop/07-y5670.png`, `desktop-no3d/04-y3240.png`) the ring text becomes noise and the center reads "REGISTER" at about 4px. Either commit to a seal drawn for small sizes or show it only large (modal).
- **Med: The static-mode hero art is weak.** Two outline 8-point stars in `--rule` behind a thin scale, with labels colliding with the star strokes (`desktop-no3d/00-y0.png`). It reads as a placeholder icon, not a hero illustration. The fallback is what reduced-motion users and many judges' laptops will see.
- **Low: The wordmark lockup is redundant and unbalanced.** Mark, then ميزان in brass Kufi, then "Mizan" in Plex Semibold, then a "Demo data" pill (`Nav.tsx:12-20`). Three name signals in roughly 140px, and the Kufi baseline sits visibly lower than the Latin (`.ar-mark{margin-top:-3px}` is a patch). The mark's star and scale strokes are the same weight, so at 34px it blurs.
- **Low: Every section is "card on paper".** The hero record card, §1 panel, §2 panel, register sheet and modal all use the same `--sheet` card with `--lift` shadow. There is no change of scale or density between sections. §4 and §5 (`desktop/07-y5670.png` to `09-y7290.png`) are competent two-column docs with H-small rules, but visually flat and long.
- **Low: The code blocks are a generic dark IDE island.** The navy `pre.code` with gold keys (`global.css:262`) is a common treatment and the darkest element on the page outside the footer. It is fine, but it does not belong to the register language (it could be a ruled ledger slip).

## Top 5 recommendations (ranked by score impact)

1. **Re-art-direct the 3D away from postcard Arabia toward an architectural, geometric register.** Drop the onion domes, palm trees and bubble clouds. Build the valley from layered paper planes cut with girih and khatam lattice (extruded `Shape` with star holes, which `shapes.ts` can already do via `archHole`). Use pointed or horseshoe arches only, from one tradition, and offset each paper layer with a visible light edge and drop shadow so it truly reads as paper cut. Use a three-value scheme per time of day: paper, ink, brass. No ochre, no glossy gold. The scale and hourglass should be matte brass on a paper plinth.
2. **Give the type a voice.** Keep Plex Sans for UI and tables. Set H1, H2 and the record names in a display face with scholarly or archival character (for example a sharp transitional serif, or a grotesk with strong humanist cuts), or go bold the other way with a condensed Kufi-inspired Latin used only for headlines. Bring ميزان up to hero scale once, as a large Kufi mark set against the headline, so the Arabic identity is felt, not just footnoted. Pair section `§` numerals with Eastern Arabic numerals (§١) as a quiet bilingual signature.
3. **Fix the hero first frame.** Replace the 0→62% gradient wash with a hard paper edge: a cut-paper mask with a deckled or ogee edge, so text sits on solid paper and the 3D lives in a clearly framed window. Raise the scale's value contrast against the background (darker plinth, lighter sky behind the beam) and redraw "Evidence/Policy" as engraved labels on the pans or as brass tags, not white sprites.
4. **Shift the palette off the stock parchment.** Keep the warm base but commit to one ownable colour that is not brass. Candidates are a deep lapis (`#1F3A5F`-ish, historically right for Islamic manuscripts) or a malachite green, used for the ink, seal and rules, with brass reserved for the seal and active nav only. Shift the paper colour toward an off-white with a slight grey-green cast so it stops reading as "warm template beige".
5. **Make the seal and the static art carry the brand when 3D is off.** Redraw the seal in two sizes: a small one with no ring text, just a star and scale at 1.5px strokes, and a large one for the modal with 10+ unit ring text. Replace the static hero `ScaleArt` with a full-bleed cut-paper illustration (an SVG version of the 3D valley, layered with offsets). Then the no-WebGL and reduced-motion experience has a real identity, not an icon.

## What would get this to 85+
The system thinking is already there: status discipline, the record/seal metaphor and the sunset as expiry. What is missing is an owned look. The 3D needs to drop the tourist-Arabia props for one rigorous geometric and architectural language that actually reads as layered cut paper, and the typography and palette need at least one choice no template would make (a display face with character, a non-brass signature colour, the Arabic mark at hero scale). Resolve the hero first frame so text and scene are cleanly separated, and make the static fallback an illustration rather than an icon. That would make this a distinctive identity, not a well-behaved template.
