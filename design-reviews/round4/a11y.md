# Mizan: mobile, accessibility and performance review (round 4, final)

**Score: 83/100** (round 3: 80, round 2: 79, round 1: 73). This is excellent, well-built accessible work with only minor issues left. All three regressions from round 3 are fixed, and I could not find a new accessibility defect. What keeps it below 85: the page is still long on phones, the HTML is rendered only on the client so the largest paint takes about 3s on a slow connection, and the phone header is tall.

## What works

- **Tablets now see content first.** The still page now applies at `(max-width: 900px), (max-height: 599px)` (`App.tsx:29`). At 768×1024 the h1 is at y=390 and the whole sample record fits on the first screen (y=608–940). At 800×700 and 900×700 the h1 is at y=390 (round 3: 717, below the fold). Tablets no longer download three.js, and the iPad-portrait page is 8.4 screens.
- **The pinned panels fit wherever 3D runs.** I scrolled through every step of §2 and §3 at 920×600, 1024×600, 1024×640, 1100×620, 1280×600, 1280×650, 1366×657 and 1440×900. Nothing was cut off and nothing sat under the header (the lowest bottom edge was 586px). The tracks are shorter (210vh/150vh), so desktop is 10.1 screens (round 3: 11.3). A new "Skip to who decides" link in the §2 panel lets keyboard users jump past both scroll tracks (tab stop 37 at 1024×640).
- **The header layout shift is gone.** `--nav-h` now defaults to the real values in CSS (98px at ≤1080, 138px at ≤340), and the ResizeObserver only fine-tunes it. The 0.0405 shift on `.hero-grid` is gone. Mobile CLS is 0.002–0.030 across two runs (round 3: 0.044–0.071), and desktop is 0.0006. Only small font-swap shifts remain.
- **Keyboard and dialog.** In slow tab runs (48 stops each at 390×844 and at 1024×640 in 3D), every stop had a visible 2px lapis ring (or the search box's `:focus-within` ring), and none was under the header or off-screen. The dialog puts focus on its `h2`, Tab goes to the reasoning summary, Shift+Tab goes to Close, the trap wraps, the background is `inert`, and Esc clears `#record/…` and returns focus to "Open record MZN-001". The rule buttons are a full-width column (3 × 258×44).
- **Contrast and automated checks.** I sampled the pixels behind the hero text over 3D at 800–1920 wide. Everything sits on solid paper, with a minimum of 5.5:1 (the caption) and 0% of pixels below 4.5:1. Text tokens are 4.93–14:1 and control borders 3.3–3.85:1. axe-core (WCAG 2.2 AA plus best practice) found 0 violations at 390, 800, 1024 and 1440, with the dialog open at 390 and 1440, and in the empty-results state.
- **Phones stay light.** No three.js. `index.js` is 91 KB gzip and the CSS 7 KB. Status messages (`role=status` for the result count and the hero record), the sr-only list of steps, the table caption and `aria-pressed` toggle groups all hold from earlier rounds.

## Top 5 recommendations (ranked by score impact)

1. **Prerender the hero and register HTML at build time.** For example, add a `renderToString` step to the Vite build, or use `vite-react-ssg`, then hydrate. Also self-host and preload Plex Sans 400 and Plex Serif 500 as woff2 files with `size-adjust` fallback faces. Expected result: LCP under 2.0s on slow 4G and no font-swap shifts.
2. **Cut the phone page to about 9 screens (8,000px or less at 390).** On ≤640px: show §4's board seats as a compact `<dl>`, fold §5's matrix into `<details>` (like Fields and Verify), merge "Next steps" into the footer, and fold the code field in each §2 step. Find out why §5 grew by 250px since round 3.
3. **Make the phone header shorter.** At ≤420px, move "Browse records" into the scrolling link row (as the first item), so the header is one 44px row plus the 42px link row (about 86px). Or hide the link row on scroll down and show it again on scroll up.
4. **Shorten §2 on desktop further** (about 30vh per step, roughly 160vh in total), bringing 1440×900 to about 9 screens.
5. **Bring the stragglers to 44px.** Header button `min-height:44px`, nav links `padding-block:12px`, "Skip to who decides" `min-height:44px; display:inline-flex; align-items:center`, and range `height:44px`.

## What would get this to 85+

Accessibility is done: I found nothing to block on in focus, the dialog, semantics, contrast, announcements or reduced motion. The last points are performance and phone length. Prerendered HTML with preloaded, metric-matched fonts (LCP under 2.5s on slow 4G), plus a phone page of 9 screens or fewer under a header of about 86px, would take this to 86–88.

## Remaining issues worth fixing

1. **The HTML is rendered only on the client, which puts LCP at about 3.0s on slow 4G.** Prerender the HTML and preload two self-hosted woff2 fonts with `size-adjust` fallbacks. This is the biggest gain left, for every visitor.
2. **The phone page is 11.5–13 screens (17 at 320px).** Fold §5's matrix, compact §4's seats, merge "Next steps" into the footer, and find out why §5 grew to 1702px.
3. **The phone header is 98px (138px at 320).** Move the header button into the link row on small screens, or hide the row while scrolling down.
4. **Some targets are below 44px.** The header button (38px), nav links (38–42px), "Skip to who decides" (21px) and the range input (28px). These pass AA, but are inconsistent with the rest of the page.
5. **The desktop §2 track is still 1890px for five cards.** Shorten it to about 160vh so the page is about 9 screens at 1440×900.
