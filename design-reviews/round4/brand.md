# Brand and visual identity review: Mizan (v4, final round)

## Score: 75/100 (round 3: 73, round 2: 68, round 1: 61)
This is strong, coherent professional identity work. The arched window, lapis ink, the 8-point star (khatam) lattice and a `§` margin grid make it recognisably Mizan. This round fixed the polish regressions cleanly, but the two items holding it below 80 are unchanged: the hero window's scene is still murky, and most of the 3D world is still plain boxes rather than cut paper.

## What works
- **The polish regressions are fixed, and fixed well.** The register's Record cell now holds the record number, type icon and name on two tight lines. Type no longer needs its own column, so the table breathes (`desktop/01-y810.png`, `02-y1620.png`). This is better than both v2 and v3. The Solidity block now wraps into readable, hand-indented lines instead of cutting off (`desktop/08-y6480.png`). The colliding "Feb 2028" tick label is gone (`desktop/05-y4050.png`). On mobile, the sunset arch now follows the §3 card instead of floating above its heading (`mobile/06-y4558.png`).
- **The identity system holds across every mode.** The pointed arch frames the 3D hero (`desktop/00-y0.png`), the still scale emblem (`mobile/00-y0.png`) and the still sunset (`desktop-no3d/03-y2430.png`). Lapis carries the buttons, `§` numbers, gates, seal, code block and verse, and brass stays on the sun, seal and star badges. Very few hackathon sites have an identity this consistent.
- **The 3D has real moments.** The close-up of the lapis gate with the brass-framed khatam "3" (`desktop/03-y2430.png`) is a confident, graphic frame that looks designed. Lattice screens casting star light on the road and the day-to-night expiry are still the strongest storytelling on the page.
- **The document sections read as a bound register.** The margin `§` numbers with a vertical hairline in §4, §5 and Next steps (`desktop/06-y4860.png`, `07-y5670.png`, `10-y8100.png`), Plex Serif headings, hairline section rules and calm two-column layouts give the long sections authority without decoration.
- **The Arabic and the verse remain the emotional anchor:** the ميزان Kufi mark at hero scale, and Al-Rahman 55:7-9 in lapis Naskh with verse markers (`desktop/10-y8100.png`).

## Problems

- **High: the hero window still holds a murky scene.** Unchanged since v3. Inside the arch: a bronze scale on a taupe plinth against taupe walls, a grey road, and a lower third that fades to flat grey (`desktop/00-y0.png`). The frame is the brand's showpiece and the first thing anyone sees, but its contents read as an underlit render. The still emblem without 3D (`mobile/00-y0.png`) is crisper.
- **High: most of the 3D world is still plain boxes.** The lattice screens and lapis gates are cut paper; the walls and buildings are flat slabs with a faint texture (`desktop/04-y3240.png`, `05-y4050.png`). The hourglass is the weakest object: smoky grey glass by day (`desktop/05-y4050.png`) and a pale ghost at night (`desktop/06-y4860.png`). The world is competent, not crafted.
- **Med: where the 3D starts and stops is still fog.** Entering §2, the scene rises out of a paper gradient that cuts through the top of the lapis gate and its star badge (`desktop/02-y1620.png`). Leaving §3, the night sky dissolves into paper through a grey band across the hourglass (`desktop/06-y4860.png`). The hero proved a hard arch edge works; these two edges have not caught up.
- **Med: two numbering styles still coexist.** §4, §5 and Next steps use the large margin `§` with a hairline. §1 (register) and the 3D §2 and §3 panels use a smaller inline `§` next to the heading (`desktop/01-y810.png`, `03-y2430.png`, `05-y4050.png`). On mobile all sections use the inline style, which is fine; on desktop the switch midway reads as two systems. The no-3D desktop (`desktop-no3d/03-y2430.png`) shows the unified version is already built.
- **Low: the "Next steps" margin column is empty.** It has the hairline but no `§` number or label, so the rule looks orphaned (`desktop/10-y8100.png`).
- **Low: "Skip to who decides" on the §2 panel** (`desktop/02-y1620.png`, `03-y2430.png`) is a small underlined utility link sitting in the brand's showcase panel. It is useful for accessibility, but it could be styled as quiet panel furniture (smaller, ink-3, at the panel's bottom rule).
- **Low: the type is still a safe single family.** Plex Serif and Plex Sans are well set, but nothing in the type is ownable. The identity rests on the arch, lattice, lapis and Kufi mark.

## Top 5 recommendations (ranked by score impact)

1. **Relight the hero window.** Make the plinth paper-white or lapis, add a warm rim light to the scale, lighten and lower the contrast of the background walls, and replace the grey bottom fade with a clean paper floor that meets the arch's base line. The goal is a crisp illustrated vignette at the quality of the §2 gate close-up.
2. **Rebuild the hourglass and the box buildings in the lattice language.** Use a flat cut-paper hourglass silhouette with brass sand, plus bevelled paper buildings with arch or star cutouts in two paper tones and lapis. Reuse `latticeShape` and the gate geometry.
3. **Give the 3D section hard edges.** Where the 3D begins below the register and ends after §3, use a straight paper edge with a soft cast shadow, or an arch-topped mask, instead of a gradient.
4. **Unify numbering on desktop.** Put §1, §2 and §3 in the same large margin `§` column as §4 and §5. For the floating 3D panels, set the numeral in the margin outside the panel. Give Next steps a margin mark, or remove its hairline.
5. **Restyle the panel utility links** ("Skip to who decides") as quiet furniture so the showcase panels stay clean.

## What would get this to 85+
The concept, palette, motifs and grid are all in place and consistent across 3D, still and mobile. The gap is now entirely the craft of the 3D world. The hero window needs a crisp, deliberately lit vignette, and the hourglass and buildings need to be rebuilt in the same cut-paper language as the lattice screens and gates, with hard paper edges where the scene starts and stops. With that, and one numbering system on desktop, this would hold up as a distinctive studio piece.

## Remaining issues worth fixing (ranked by impact)
1. **Hero window scene:** murky lighting and the grey fade inside the arch (`desktop/00-y0.png`). It is the first frame and the brand's showpiece.
2. **Hourglass and box buildings:** not in the cut-paper language. The hourglass reads as grey glass by day and a ghost at night (`desktop/05-y4050.png`, `06-y4860.png`).
3. **Fog edges where the 3D starts and stops** (`desktop/02-y1620.png`, `06-y4860.png`): replace them with hard paper edges.
4. **Two `§` numbering styles on desktop:** inline on §1 to §3, margin column on §4, §5 and Next steps. Use the margin column everywhere, as the no-3D layout already does.
5. **Small furniture:** the empty margin rule on Next steps, and the loud "Skip to who decides" link inside the §2 panel.
